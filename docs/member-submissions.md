# Member submissions

Tracking submissions follow the supplied tracking-number form. Each reserved deal can have one entry per unique product UPC, with account email, payment-account name, tracking/drop-off references, quantity, destination, and optional Amazon delivery OTP. Multiple references should be separated by comma and space. Additional UPCs can be added while reserved units remain. Total submitted units cannot exceed the reservation, including concurrent requests.

Shipping label requests follow the supplied label form: member email, WhatsApp phone/Discord tag, reserved item, origin ZIP, quantity in each box, and matching pound weights. Deal posters choose whether labels are available and a minimum total quantity per request. The database enforces eligibility, the minimum, positive matching box arrays, and reserved-unit limits.

The super admin reviews requests in **Label requests** and sets Requested, In progress, or Completed. Members can edit requests only while Requested. Labels are prepared separately; the app does not generate/purchase carrier labels or currently email form-response copies. Posting-only admins cannot access submissions or label requests. Existing legacy delivery references are preserved.
