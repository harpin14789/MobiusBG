# Member submissions

Tracking submissions follow the supplied tracking-number form. Each reserved deal can have one entry per unique product UPC, with account email, payment-account name, tracking/drop-off references, quantity, destination, and optional Amazon delivery OTP. Multiple references should be separated by comma and space. Additional UPCs can be added while reserved units remain. Total submitted units cannot exceed the reservation, including concurrent requests.

Members can choose **Edit reservation** in My reservations to change their total units, including zero to cancel. Changes update group capacity in the same transaction. Units cannot be removed below the quantity already included in delivery submissions, received, or in a shipping-label request; legacy deliveries without itemized quantities retain their existing reservation quantity. A closed or deleted deal cannot gain units. If payout terms have changed, the member can reserve additional units separately at current terms, preserving the original reservation's terms. A cancelled reservation remains in the history with zero units.

The super admin can edit a member's quantity and the saved base/commission for a specific reservation from its member profile or the reservation breakdown. Payout terms cannot change after any units are received or paid, and the same delivery, label, capacity, and closed-deal limits apply. The affected member receives an email about a super-admin change. New reservations verify the displayed price against the current member-specific terms before reserving, so a stale browser cannot silently accept a different payout.

From a deal's **Set member pricing** action, the super admin can set a private base payout and commission for one member. This affects that member's future reservations only; other members keep the standard price and existing reservations keep their saved terms. Restore standard pricing by removing the override. Members cannot read another member's private terms, and posting-only admins cannot modify them.

Shipping label requests follow the supplied label form: member email, WhatsApp phone/Discord tag, reserved item, origin ZIP, quantity in each box, and matching pound weights. Deal posters choose whether labels are available and a minimum total quantity per request. The database enforces eligibility, the minimum, positive matching box arrays, and reserved-unit limits.

The super admin reviews requests in **Label requests** and sets Requested, In progress, or Completed. Members can edit requests only while Requested. Labels are prepared separately; the app does not generate/purchase carrier labels or currently email form-response copies. Posting-only admins cannot access submissions or label requests. Existing legacy delivery references are preserved.
